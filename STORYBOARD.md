# Nyāya-sūtra, methodically: storyboard (as built, 19 scenes)

Style: palm-leaf manuscript (parchment, ink, red rubrication). Guide: **Akṣapāda**, an old teacher who keeps asking "why?" (Akṣapāda is a traditional epithet of Gautama).
Spine: the Nyāya-sūtra in order, adhyāya by adhyāya. Woven in: the **Logic lab** (inference engine, vyāpti, fallacies) and the **Sabhā** (debate arena: vāda / jalpa / vitaṇḍā, chala, jāti, nigrahasthāna).
Rank ladder (the "accessory"): Jijñāsu → Pramātā → Tārkika → Vādin → Naiyāyika.
Source text: GRETIL "Gautama: Nyāyasūtra" (Sansknet input, not proof-read; 533 sūtras in this edition, other editions count ~528 to 539). Parsed copy in `data/`.

## Sourcing and caveats (shown in-app)
- Sūtra text: GRETIL (Sansknet), unproofed, word boundaries are sloppy; sūtra numbers follow that edition.
- Dating shown as ranges: sūtra from 6th c. BCE to 2nd c. CE (Wikipedia) or c. 200 CE (IEP); Vātsyāyana c. 450; Uddyotakara c. 600; Vācaspati Miśra c. 900; Udayana c. 975; Gaṅgeśa c. 1325 (IEP).
- The sūtra's five hetvābhāsa are not the later textbook five; the app shows both and says so.
- Navya-Nyāya vs predicate logic: scholars disagree (Ingalls, Matilal, Ganeri); we present it as comparison, not equivalence.
- Vātsyāyana's examples for some fallacies and for the three anumāna types still need checking against a translation before I commit them to narration.
