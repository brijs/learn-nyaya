Nyāya-sūtra, methodically

An animated, narrated walk through Gautama’s Nyāya-sūtra, adhyāya by adhyāya: the sixteen topics, the pramāṇas, the five-limbed argument, vyāpti, the faulty reasons, and the art of debate. With a logic lab and a debate arena.

Narration: Microsoft neural voices. Sūtras are read by a Hindi voice from the Devanagari, so Sanskrit sounds are approximate. The on-screen text is the authority.
